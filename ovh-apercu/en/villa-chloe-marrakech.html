Your questions.

Is furniture included in the price?

The villa price includes the fitted kitchen, appliances, home automation, air conditioning, landscaped garden and 13 m pool. Furniture is excluded. An optional furnishing service is available at an additional cost, subject to quotation.

Is the kitchen open to the living room?

The main kitchen is a large, enclosed, separate room. A distinct kitchenette is planned beside a large window facing the pool.

Who builds my villa?

Williams & Harper builds the villas within the development. Each model has a defined architecture and design that cannot be changed by the buyer, preserving the estate’s consistency. The only layout adaptation offered is to replace one of the two living rooms with an additional guest bedroom, at no extra cost.

What are the timelines and payment terms?

These depend on the selected option and contractual schedule. Timelines shown on the site are indicative; your offer and contract provide the detailed payment schedule.
