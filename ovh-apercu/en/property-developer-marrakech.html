01Who we are

A property developer in Marrakech.

Williams & Harper is behind Domaine Red Stone, a private villa development in Marrakech. Chloé, Céline and Margaux offer three ways to live within one estate, with contemporary architecture and gardens at the heart of the design.

02Our role

A villa development with a defined architectural identity.

Domaine Red Stone is a villa development within a private, secure gated community. You choose your villa — Chloé, Céline or Margaux — from the models and locations shown on the master plan. Williams & Harper is responsible for design and construction. Each model has a fixed architectural design that cannot be changed by the buyer. The only layout adaptation offered is to replace one of the two living rooms with an additional guest bedroom, at no extra cost.

03Supporting you

From your choice of villa to the handover of keys.

From choosing your villa model and its location on the master plan to receiving the keys, we support you at every stage. We explain the planned specifications, purchase terms and construction process, in keeping with your chosen model and the estate’s architectural identity.
