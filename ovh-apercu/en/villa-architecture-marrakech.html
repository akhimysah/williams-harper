Before it becomes an address,

01 — The idea

Imagining another
way of living.

Williams & Harper began with a desire to create places where architecture, materials and landscape form a single composition. Before choosing finishes, we imagine the spaces: a living room opening onto a garden, a view over water, a place to come together.

02 — The drawing

Giving purpose
to every line.

Drawing explores proportions, solids and voids. It seeks a balance between a sheltering roof, a window that welcomes light and a material that adds depth. Gradually, an idea becomes an architectural language.

03 — Living architecture

From architecture
to everyday moments.

The project goes beyond the façade, into gardens, reflections and daily discoveries. Our ambition is to create a setting for what matters: welcoming, sharing, growing and feeling at home.
