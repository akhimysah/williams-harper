Villa Margaux

Discover Villa Margaux, an architectural masterpiece nestled within the prestigious Red Stone estate in Marrakech.

Designed by two visionary designers for Williams & Harper, this spacious villa offers over 711 square meters of living space, complemented by 370 square meters of covered terraces, on a generous plot of over 2,000 square meters. Enjoy panoramic views of the Atlas Mountains.

Immerse yourself in an exceptional realm with six spacious suites, each designed to provide optimal comfort while ensuring the privacy of its occupants. Villa Margaux also features a stunning glass private elevator that combines modernity with elegance.

Les terrasses spacieuses sont idéales pour des moments de détente en plein air ou pour des réceptions élégantes sous le ciel de Marrakech.
