Red Stone Estate · As a family

A home to grow up in

A gated, guarded estate, a park at its heart, villas with four to six suites. Red Stone is designed for families who want space, quiet, and children playing outside.

As a family

Children outside, parents at ease

On the estate, children walk to the playground, cycle to the café and come home along the palm-lined lanes. A single entrance, guarded day and night, and calm internal roads.

The playground

A garden to play in

A slide, timber climbing frames, a shade sail and stone benches, in an enclosed garden below the padel courts. The older ones move on to padel, parents watch from the benches or the café terrace.

Reserved for residents and their guests. Lit in the evening.

The home

The kitchen, the heart of the home

In all three villas the kitchen opens onto the double-height living room and the terrace: you cook together, keep an eye on the pool, and dine outside from spring onwards. Four to six suites, each with its own bathroom, so that everyone has their room, grandparents included.

All three villas
The garden

One to two thousand square metres of your own

Each villa has its own enclosed garden, pool and private entrance. Grass to run on, a pond, an olive tree, and a wall that protects without closing in. The garden is delivered planted, with recycled irrigation.

Pool fence or pool alarm fitted on request before handover.

The amenities

After school, at the weekend

The café, padel and the quiet of a library: enough to fill a Wednesday or a Sunday without leaving the estate.

Around the estate

Schools, healthcare, weekends

Marrakech has what a family expects of a big city, a quarter of an hour from the estate.

Schools
International schools following the French, British and American curricula, from nursery to the baccalaureate, fifteen to twenty-five minutes from the estate.
Healthcare
Private clinics, paediatricians and duty pharmacies in Guéliz and Hivernage, thirteen kilometres away.
Weekends
The golf courses, the Agafay desert, the Ourika valley, the Majorelle garden: half-day outings, back in time for dinner.
Airport
Six kilometres away, three hours' flight from Paris, Brussels or Amsterdam: grandparents come for the weekend.
Concierge
Childcare, padel coach, swimming lessons, groceries delivered to the villa: arranged on request by the concierge.
Security
A single entrance guarded 24 hours a day, CCTV in the common areas, an enclosed, lit playground.
Parents' questions

What families ask us

Which villa, which school, how safe: answers to the questions we are asked most often.

Ask a question
01Which villa for a large family?

Villa Margaux, 711 m² and six suites, for large families or live-in grandparents. Villa Céline, 607 m² and five suites, for a family with three children. Villa Chloé, 473 m² and four suites, for a family with two children and a guest room.

02Is the estate safe for children?

The estate is fenced, with a single entrance guarded 24 hours a day and access control. Common areas are under CCTV, internal roads are limited to walking pace, and the playground is enclosed.

03Are there international schools nearby?

Yes. Marrakech has schools following the French, British and American curricula, from nursery to the baccalaureate, fifteen to twenty-five minutes from the estate. The concierge can arrange school transport.

04Can we come only during the school holidays?

Yes. The concierge maintains the villa, garden and pool while you are away, prepares the house before you arrive and, if you wish, lets it for the rest of the year.
