A year at Red Stone

Four seasons, one sky

Over three hundred days of sunshine a year. Mild winters facing the snow-capped Atlas, dry summers with long evenings. What you do, month after month.

December to February

Winter, the season of the villas

Twenty degrees in the afternoon, the white Atlas at the end of the garden, breakfast outside. It is the season when Marrakech is most alive: golf, terraces, year-end celebrations. In the evening, the fireplace.

Skiing at Oukaïmeden, 75 km away, from January to March.

March to May

Spring, everything in bloom

The estate gardens are at their best: agapanthus, jasmine, orange trees in blossom. Twenty-five degrees, the first swims, cycling under the olive trees. The Easter holidays are spent outdoors.

The Ourika valley and the Ouzoud falls, at the weekend.

June to September

Summer, in the evening hours

Hot and dry, without humidity. The day is lived early and late: pool in the morning, a siesta in the cool of the stone, dinner on the terrace as the sun drops behind the Atlas. The home automation sets the air conditioning before you return.

Essaouira and the ocean, two and a half hours away, for the hottest days.

October to November

Autumn, the golden light

The regulars' favourite season. Twenty-eight degrees in October, the golf courses reopened, evenings still mild on the terrace. The city finds its rhythm again, the restaurants their terraces.

The Agafay desert, a night under the stars, forty minutes away.

Visiting

Choose your season

Visits to the estate all year round, by appointment. We recommend the late afternoon, when the light falls on the Atlas.
