Red Stone by night

When the stone lights up

In the evening, light takes over from the sun: lines beneath the roofs, lit pools, the Atlas in shadow. The villas change their face.

Orchestrated light

Designed like the architecture

Each villa is delivered with its lighting plan: strips built into the roof overhangs, marble pillars lit at a grazing angle, gardens and pools marked at ground level, interiors in scenes adjustable from the home automation.

The estate in the evening

Roads lit at ground level

No street lamps: the lanes are marked by low bollards and the palms are lit from below. The sky stays dark, the stars visible, and the villas cannot see each other.
