The maths

Two to three times more villa

For the same budget, Marrakech offers two to three times the floor area of Marbella and up to five times that of the Côte d'Azur, with one to two thousand square metres of land, an infinity pool and an unobstructed view of the Atlas.

And a three-hour flight from Paris, Brussels or Amsterdam, with no time difference in winter.
