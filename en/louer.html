Letting your villa

Your villa works while you are away

The estate concierge handles the letting of your villa while you are away, from the listing to the inventory. You choose the dates, it does the rest.

How it works

Four steps, one contact

A management mandate signed with the concierge, a shared calendar, a monthly statement. You keep control of the periods when the villa stays yours.

  1. The mandate and the calendar

    You indicate the weeks you keep for yourself. The rest is open to letting, on the terms you set: minimum stay, number of guests, pets or not.

  2. Putting it on the market

    Professional photos, listing on the platforms and with our direct clientele, seasonal pricing, replies to enquiries within 24 hours.

  3. Welcome and stay

    Keys handed over by the concierge, cleaning, linen, pool and garden maintenance during the stay, assistance 7 days a week. Your personal belongings are stored in a locked room.

  4. Statement and transfer

    Each month, a statement of nights let, income and costs. The balance is transferred to your convertible-dirham account, transferable abroad.

The calendar

Two high seasons, a quiet summer

Marrakech lets from October to May, with peaks at Christmas, February and Easter holidays. Summer is quieter: it is often the period owners keep for themselves, or open to longer stays.

Villas with four to six suites and a private pool: demand comes from families and groups of friends, for a week on average.

What the concierge does

Everything, except decide for you

There is one concierge for the whole estate. It knows your villa, its settings and its habits, and it is on site at all times.

Upkeep
Pool, garden, air conditioning, home automation: checked every week, let or not.
Home
Keys, inventory, presentation of the villa, emergency number. In French, English and Arabic.
Cleaning and linen
Between each stay and, at the guests' request, during the stay. Household linen provided and laundered.
Guest services
Private chef, airport transfers, excursions, babysitting: on commission, at no cost to you.
Reporting
Monthly statement, photos after each stay, annual inventory.
Tax
Rental income is taxed in Morocco after an allowance; tax treaties avoid double taxation. Our partner adviser supports you.
