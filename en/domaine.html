Red Stone Estate · Marrakech

Eight hectares, forty-one villas

A private, fenced and guarded address, drawn around a central park. Three villa signatures, one architectural language.

The estate

A private neighbourhood, drawn in one stroke

The Red Stone estate is organised around a central avenue and a landscaped park. Forty-one villas, each with pool and garden, oriented to preserve privacy and the view of the Atlas.

8 haEnclosed, guarded land
41Villas, three signatures
1,000 to 2,000 m²Of land per villa
5 minFrom the airport
The entrance
A single guarded access, with drop-off in front of the concierge and visitor parking.
The central avenue
Lined with palm trees, it serves the plots and leads to the park.
The park
At the heart of the estate: pools, winding paths, a playground, padel courts.
The amenities
Red Coffee, store and gym, grouped near the entrance.
The plots
From 1,000 to 2,000 m², each villa with its garden, pool and private access.
The networks
Fibre optics, central satellite, recycled irrigation, automated street lighting.
The park

A garden at the centre

The landscape architect drew the park as an enlarged villa garden: pools, lawns, curved paths under the palms, a children's playground and the padel courts on its edge. The villas open onto it without being exposed.

Recycled-water irrigation, low-water Mediterranean species.

Commitments

Built to last

The same standards for all forty-one villas, from structure to finishes.

  • Solar panels and double glazing on every villa
  • Thermal and acoustic insulation, seismic standard
  • Compresseur centralisé externe : climatisation et chauffage silencieux
  • Recycled-water irrigation and automated outdoor lighting
  • Fibre optique, récepteur satellite centralisé, local technique séparé
  • Designed by Line Design, site supervision by the Williams & Harper team
