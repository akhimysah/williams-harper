The materials

What you touch, every day

Stone, marble, wood, brass, bronze. The villas are built with materials that age well and are a pleasure to touch.

Eight materials

Chosen to last

All natural, all present in the three villas. The Line Design catalogue interiors combine them into moods; here, each one in close-up.

Line Design

The materials, staged

The Line Design studio composed the interiors of the three villas from this palette: marble floors, stone or walnut walls, warm light, touches of brass. The catalogue presents the moods room by room, from living room to dressing room.

The interiors catalogue
And outside

Travertine, wood, water

The terraces are in pale travertine, the outdoor ceilings in wood slats with light strips, the pools in black stone. The same materials inside and out: the villa does not stop at the glass wall.
