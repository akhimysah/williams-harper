Villa Céline

Discover Villa Céline, a prestigious address within the Red Stone estate in Marrakech.

Designed by a renowned duo of architects from Williams & Harper, this villa stands out with its meticulously arranged 607 square meters of living space and 177 square meters of covered terraces, offering a refined and intimate living experience. Set on over 1,500 square meters of land, it offers an elegant and exclusive setting.

This unique residence features five spacious suites, each designed to provide unparalleled comfort and perfect intimacy. Among the property's many highlights, its private elevator seamlessly blends functionality with aesthetics, facilitating access between the various levels of the villa.

Les terrasses spacieuses invitent à profiter du climat ensoleillé de Marrakech et à admirer les vues panoramiques, garantissant des moments de détente dans un environnement paisible et privé.
