Frequently asked questions

What we are asked most

Buying, handover, customisation, management: answers to the questions we are asked most often.

Ask a question
01Can a non-resident buy a villa in Morocco?

Yes. Foreign nationals may freely purchase titled property in Morocco, agricultural land excepted. The Red Stone estate is fully titled and each villa has its own land title. Our team guides you through every formality with the notary.

02Are the villas delivered turnkey?

Yes. Each villa is delivered finished: fitted kitchen, bathrooms, dressing rooms, home automation, air conditioning and heating, solar panels, pool and landscaped garden. The interior schemes shown in the catalogue are quoted separately by our designers.

03Can the plans be customised?

Within the structure, yes: choice of materials, layout of the suites, a bar or home cinema in place of a bedroom, garden design. Changes are studied with the architect before signing.

04Is the estate secured and managed?

The estate is fenced, guarded around the clock with access control, and run by a concierge service that maintains the common areas and gardens and, on request, your villa while you are away.

05Can I rent out my villa?

Yes. The concierge can manage rentals of your villa for the periods you are not using it, from welcoming guests to upkeep.

06How do I visit?

By appointment, seven days a week, at the estate or by video call for a first presentation. Write to us or call +212 6 17 17 47 78.
